# CS-02 Damage, missing goods and claims

*Corlaan Logistics, customer service procedure. Owner: claims coordinator. Version 2026-10. Fictional.*

## Recognise a claim

Treat an email as a possible claim when the customer reports damaged, wet, crushed or missing goods, says a delivery "never arrived" while the TMS shows it delivered, or contests a signature.

## What to do the same day

1. Do **not** send a status line or the POD as an answer.
2. Forward the email to the **claims** queue with the shipment number.
3. Reply to the customer within 4 working hours: acknowledge, ask for photos of the goods and the packaging, and the CMR with the reservation written on it.
4. Damage must be reported on the CMR at delivery, or **within 7 days in writing** for damage that was not visible (CMR Convention, art. 30). Mention this deadline to the customer.
5. Never admit liability or promise compensation in writing. The claims coordinator decides after the survey.

## Amounts

- Up to €500: the claims coordinator decides alone.
- Above €500: the operations manager approves.
- Legal letters (lawyer, formal notice): go to the team lead and the operations manager the same day; no reply from customer service.
