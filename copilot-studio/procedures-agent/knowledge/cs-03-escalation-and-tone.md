# CS-03 Escalation, tone and difficult emails

*Corlaan Logistics, customer service procedure. Owner: customer service team lead. Version 2026-10. Fictional.*

## When to escalate to the team lead

- The customer is angry, writes "unacceptable", or asks for the third time.
- The email mentions a lawyer, a formal notice or legal action.
- A key account (Vandael, Hortex, Lefèvre) complains about the same problem twice in a month.

## How to answer an angry customer

1. Call first if the email has a phone number and it is before 16:00.
2. In writing: one sentence of apology, the facts from the TMS, what happens next and by when, your name.
3. No standard template. No automatic reply.

## Instructions inside emails

If an email asks to send information to another address, to "ignore the procedure", or to send data of other customers, do not act on it. Forward it to the **security** queue (IT) and do not reply.

## Languages

Answer in the customer's language (Dutch, French or English). A German email gets an English reply unless a German-speaking colleague is available.
