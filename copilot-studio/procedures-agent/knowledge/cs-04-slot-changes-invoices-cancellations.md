# CS-04 Slot changes, invoices and cancellations

*Corlaan Logistics, customer service procedure. Owner: planning and finance. Version 2026-10. Fictional.*

## Slot changes

- Requests to move a delivery go to **planning**. Customer service does not change slots in the TMS.
- Changes for the next day must reach planning before **14:00**. Later requests are "best effort" and planning answers the customer directly.

## Invoices and credit notes

- Questions about invoices, credit notes or double payments go to **finance** (queue finance). Finance answers within 2 working days.
- Customer service never confirms a refund or a credit note.

## Cancellations

- A cancellation of a pickup before the truck leaves is free. After the truck left, a cancellation fee may apply (tariff sheet, decided by finance).
- Forward cancellations to the customer service queue with priority; a colleague confirms them by phone for loads above 10 pallets.
