    Assumptions

    What moves the 3-year NPV most (±25%)

    Trust is earned in steps

    1. 0 · Shadow

      Runs on a copy of the mailbox; outcomes only in the log. Gate: agrees with agents on ≥ 90% of in-scope emails.

    2. 1 · Draft-only

      Every reply is a draft with a Teams card. Gate: ≥ 95% sent unedited, no data-protection rejections, DPO sign-off.

    3. 2 · Graduated

      Automatic for plain status and POD to contacts. Weekly review of 20 automatic replies.

    4. 3 · Expand

      Phase 2: slot changes. Separate design and business case.

    Going down is one setting, and automatic after a data-protection incident, a harmful reply in the weekly sample or a failed evaluation after a model update.

    What this demo is not

    • The reading step is a deterministic stand-in with the same JSON contract as the Copilot Studio agent. A real model must pass its own evaluation first.
    • The Copilot Studio agent and the Power Automate flow are a design and a build guide, not exports from a tenant.
    • No real emails, customers, pilot or results. Every figure is an illustrative assumption.
