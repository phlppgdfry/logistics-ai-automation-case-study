# Inspiration session for management (45 minutes)

Before discovery there is often a session with the management team that has heard of "Copilot" and wants to know what it means for them. The goal is not to sell a tool. It is to leave with **one process worth a discovery workshop**, an owner, and realistic expectations.

## Agenda

| Min | Block | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Their question | Ask each manager: *"Where does your team copy information from one screen to another?"* Write it down. |
| 5–15 | Three kinds of help | **Copilot in Microsoft 365** (personal productivity: mail, meetings, documents) · **an agent built in Copilot Studio** (a specific task with company data) · **automation without AI** (Power Automate: rules, approvals, integrations). Most value comes from the second and third together. |
| 15–25 | One demo, one refusal | Show the [prototype](https://phlppgdfry.github.io/logistics-ai-automation-case-study/demo/?tour): a status email answered from the TMS, and the same email from a stranger, refused. *"The model reads; the rules decide."* |
| 25–35 | What it costs and what it takes | Licences and Copilot credits in round numbers ([business case](../05-business-case/business-case.md)); data that must be in order first; a team lead who owns the rules; 8–10 weeks to a safe pilot. |
| 35–45 | Pick one | Score their ideas from minute 0 on value and feasibility. Pick one for a 2-hour [discovery workshop](discovery-workshop.md); name the owner and the date. |

## Things to say plainly

- **"It will be wrong sometimes."** That is why it starts as drafts, with a test set and a person who reviews.
- **"The value is time, not heads."** Here the cash comes from not hiring temps and not adding a hire. Saying that early prevents a team that fears the project.
- **"Your data decides how good it is."** If the TMS has no recent event for a subcontractor shipment, no model can make the answer right.
- **"Some ideas do not need AI."** A form, a portal link or a plain flow is often cheaper and more reliable.

## Leave behind

One page: the three kinds of help, the demo link, the chosen process with its owner and date, and the questions the discovery workshop will answer.
